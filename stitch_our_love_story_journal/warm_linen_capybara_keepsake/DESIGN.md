## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes the artisanal tactile charm of bespoke wedding stationery with the tender intimacy of a personal memory journal. Targeted at couples, sentimental archivists, and romantic storytellers, the visual environment fosters unhurried calm, profound warmth, and enduring nostalgia. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Tactile Minimalism infused with Editorial Classicism**. Interfaces evoke handmade cotton rag paper, fine deckle edges, blind debossing, and delicate warm foil linings. Visual depth relies on realistic pressed-paper textures and soft fiber layers rather than stark digital constructs. Whimsical line-art capybara vignettes—drawn with vintage botanical lithograph precision—act as subtle, quiet companions across headers and empty states, infusing grounded levity without sacrificing refined romance.

## Colors

The color architecture is built entirely upon organic, culinary, and artisanal pigments that recreate the physical sensations of heavy linen paper, roasted hazelnut ink, and warm milk coffee.

- **Primary (`#4A3B32` - Hazelnut Roast):** The dominant ink color for high-contrast typography, formal headers, and structural lines. Never use pure black (`#000000`); hazelnut ink preserves warmth and historical richness.
- **Secondary (`#8C6D58` - Warm Latte):** Used for supporting metadata, editorial captions, secondary interactive icons, and subtle dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#C29B38` - Antique Gold Foil):** Reserved for hairline accents, wax-seal emblems, foil highlights, and premium callouts. Applied with restraint to mirror genuine hot-stamping foil.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FDFBF7` - Creamy Linen):** The base canvas. Emulates unbleached archival deckle-edge rag paper.
- **Supporting Tones:**
  - Parchment Layer / Container Base: `#EFE6DD`
  - Soft Sepia Border / Muted Stroke: `#D7C4B7`
  - Deep Espresso Anchor: `#362A23`

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between classic ceremonial invitation scripts and readable contemporary digital ergonomics.

- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display introduces literary weight, dramatic editorial contrast, and graceful calligraphic curves. Use italic variants generously for sentimental prompts, quotes, dates, and poetic excerpts.
- **Body & Functional UI:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides clean, friendly, open-aperture contrast that keeps lengthy diary entries, dates, and form labels crisp and effortlessly legible.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase metadata (such as timestamps, section markers, or card rubrics) set in Plus Jakarta Sans should use wide letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to replicate engraved stationery plate lettering.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first around a disciplined 4-column structure expanding to 8 columns on tablet and 12 columns on desktop, anchored by an intimate maximum reading canvas of `760px` for memory narratives and invitations.

- **Rhythm & Breathing Room:** Generous vertical rhythm mimics the generous margins of bespoke stationery. Content blocks avoid dense clumping; sections are separated by at least `space-xl` (2.5rem).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - *Mobile (< 640px):* Outer margin is `1.25rem`. Cards flow in a single stack with deckle-edge bottom seams and full-width card structures.
  - *Tablet (640px - 1024px):* Outer margin relaxes to `2rem`. Two-column asymmetric scrapbook layouts activate for photographic memories and letter extracts.
  - *Desktop (> 1024px):* Outer margin expands to `3.5rem`. Layout conforms to a centered journal spread with fixed stationery borders and soft ambient outer margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh digital drop shadows in favor of paper stratification, fiber layering, and physical debossing.

- **Paper Layering:** Elevation is primarily achieved via tonal contrast. The canvas uses `#FDFBF7` (creamy linen), while floating cards and memo slips sit atop `#EFE6DD` with a feather-soft ambient contact shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Gold Foil Hairlines:** Elevated cards or featured announcements receive a delicate double hairline border: an inner solid 1px stroke in `#D7C4B7` inset by 3px from an outer faint gold border in `#C29B38` at 40% opacity.
- **Debossed Depths:** Form fields, quote wells, and empty memory slots use a subtle inset letterpress shadow (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.08)`) with a faint bottom highlight (`0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).
- **Wax Seals & Medallions:** Critical call-to-actions and keepsake badges utilize deep warm-toned elevation (`0 6px 14px -3px rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.22)`) paired with radial gradients that replicate molded sealing wax.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, tailored shape language (`roundedness: 1`), keeping corner radiuses soft (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`) to echo hand-cut stationery and deckle-edged stock rather than hyper-rounded app widgets.

- **Torn & Deckle Edges:** Key component tops or bottoms feature subtle, organic deckle silhouettes (achieved via CSS masking or bespoke SVG clipping paths), hinting at hand-torn cotton watercolor paper.
- **Notched & Scalloped Corners:** Tags, photo frames, and stamps use subtle 4px notched corners or delicate postage-stamp scalloped edges.
- **Wax Seals:** Exclusively circular or organically hand-stamped asymmetrical circles (`rounded-full`), providing a warm, human contrast to rectangular paper cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Wax Emblems
- **Primary Buttons:** Styled as pressed hazelnut cardstock (`#4A3B32`) with creamy linen text (`#FDFBF7`), set in Plus Jakarta Sans Medium with slight tracking. Hover induces a soft gold glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #C29B38`.
- **Secondary / Letterpress Buttons:** Set with transparent backgrounds, 1px border in `#8C6D58`, and text in `#4A3B32`. On active tap, background settles into `#EFE6DD`.
- **Wax Seal Action Buttons:** Circular floating action triggers styled in deep burnt carmine or golden ochre (`#A64B2A` or `#C29B38`), debossed with a subtle capybara crest or monogram motif in the center.

### Stationery Cards & Memory Slips
- Surfaces feature creamy linen background `#FDFBF7` bordered by a 1px `#D7C4B7` hairline.
- A delicate botanical capybara watermark is placed at 8% opacity in the bottom-right quadrant of empty or reflective state cards.
- Cards may include an optional simulated brass paperclip or washi-tape header accent at the top center.

### Text Inputs & Journal Entry Fields
- Inputs emulate ruled journal notebooks: transparent background with a single baseline border in `#D7C4B7` instead of a harsh enclosing box.
- Placeholder text is set in Playfair Display italic (`#8C6D58` at 60% opacity).
- Focused state transitions the baseline to `#4A3B32` with a soft amber ambient ring.

### Chips, Tags & Date Badges
- Modeled after tied luggage gift tags or vintage botanical labels.
- Pale linen background (`#EFE6DD`) with a subtle 1px border (`#D7C4B7`), bound with uppercase tracked typography (`label-md`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes appear as hand-drawn squares with soft corners; checked states display a hand-inked cross or checkmark in `#4A3B32`.
- Radio buttons feature an outer ring in `#8C6D58` with a centered solid hazelnut dot upon selection.

### Capybara Watermarks & Illustrated Accents
- Minimalist, fine-line ink vignettes depicting serene capybaras with floral crowns, resting beside tea cups, or peacefully soaking with yuzu fruit.
- Watermarks must never exceed 10% opacity behind reading copy to ensure strict WCAG AAA contrast compliance for foreground text.
