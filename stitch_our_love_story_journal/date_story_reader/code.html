A Letter Written By You

Under the Milky Way, Wrapped in Your Brown Sweater

I still remember the salt air in your hair when we climbed onto the sand dunes. You were shivering so I wrapped my arms around you inside that thick wool sweater. We didn’t talk for almost twenty minutes—just watching the Pacific waves crash against the Golden Gate fog.

“You whispered that this felt like forever, and I knew right then I wanted every forever with you.”

The bonfires down the shoreline had died out into glowing cedar embers, leaving just us and the endless tide. When you turned around to laugh at how soaked my cuffs had gotten, your eyes caught the amber harbor beacon—a memory that is forever embossed into my heart.

Yours always, Julian • St. 14
